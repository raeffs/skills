# A router over the upstream catalogue

`ask-matt` is not adopted. It asks which skill or flow fits your situation and answers from the full mattpocock/skills catalogue. Requests to adopt it, or to adapt it into a router over this repo, are out of scope.

## Why this is out of scope

The skill is a routing map over Matt's catalogue. Every answer it gives names one of his skills and the flow between them. This repo adopts a small subset of that catalogue and changes some of what it adopts, so the map is wrong here on both counts: it points at skills that do not exist in this repo, and it does not know the ones that do.

A router over this repo would have to be written from scratch and kept in step with each adoption and each deviation. The skill descriptions already do that job: each one says what the skill does and when to use it, and the harness matches on them.
