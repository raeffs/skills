# Skills that upstream has frozen

Skills under `skills/misc/` in mattpocock/skills are not adopted. That covers `git-guardrails-claude-code`, `migrate-to-shoehorn`, `scaffold-exercises`, `setup-pre-commit`, and anything that lands there later. Requests to adopt one are out of scope.

## Why this is out of scope

Upstream has frozen `misc/`. The skills there do not ship in their plugin, have no docs page, and every issue against them is closed. The maintainer does not run them and does not fix them. See `.out-of-scope/frozen-misc-skills.md` in the upstream repo.

An adopted skill is kept in step with its upstream. A frozen upstream gives nothing to keep in step with, so adopting one of these means taking on its maintenance alone. If a skill moves out of `misc/` into `engineering/` or `productivity/`, it is back in scope from that point.
